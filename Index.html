ஆய்வு & பரிந்துரைகள் · Analysis & Suggestions

Rules used: Dina (remainder 2,4,6,8,0), Ganam, Mahendram (4,7,10…25), Sthree Deergam (boy's star ≥7th from girl's; ≥13 excellent), Yoni (enemy pairs fail), Rasi (boy's rasi 7th or more, or same rasi), Rasi Adhipathi (lords not enemies), Vasiyam, Rajju (different group) and Vedha (not a vedha pair). Regional almanacs vary on a few of these, so the astrologer's judgement governs.
